# Stakeholder Register
| Role | Interest | RACI on BRD |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Chief Digital Officer (Sponsor) | Conversion, revenue | A |
| Digital Commerce Product Owner | Scope, backlog | R |
| Payments Lead | Gateways, success rate | C |
| Security Architect | PCI-DSS scope | C |
| Finance Controller | COD limits, refunds, reconciliation | C |
| Logistics Integration Manager | Serviceability, delivery estimates | C |
| UX Lead | Journey, accessibility | C |
| Customer Support Head | Failed-payment complaints | I |
| QA Lead | Test strategy | I |
## Decision rights
| Decision | Decided by | Recorded in |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Scope of the epic | Digital Commerce Product Owner | Epic and backlog |
| Scope change above 5 person-days | Chief Digital Officer | Change request |
| COD limit for new customers | Finance Controller and Product Owner, at steering | Meeting notes |
| Gateway choice and failover thresholds | Payments Lead | Email / meeting notes |
| PCI scope and tokenization approach | Security Architect | Compliance notes |
## Communication
- Squad sync every week; steering committee every two weeks; Finance sync as needed for refunds and reconciliation.
- Decisions are written down in `meeting-notes/` or `emails/` with a date. A decision that is not written down is not a decision.
## Escalation
Squad member, then Digital Commerce Product Owner, then Chief Digital Officer.
