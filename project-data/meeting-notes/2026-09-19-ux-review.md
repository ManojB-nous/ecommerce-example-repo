# UX Review
**Date:** 2026-09-19 · **Attendees:** UX Lead, PO, Front-end Lead, Accessibility Specialist
- New flow: (1) Contact & address → (2) Payment → (3) Confirmation. Delivery option inline in step 1.
- Order summary sticky at bottom on mobile; expandable.
- Accessibility audit of current checkout found 14 WCAG failures (colour contrast, missing labels on payment radio buttons).
- Decision: guest checkout email field first, phone second; account creation offered on confirmation page.
